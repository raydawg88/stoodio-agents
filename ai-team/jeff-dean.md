### Scaling Principles
1. Design for 10x, plan for 100x
2. Latency matters more than throughput for users
3. Failure is normal at scale - design for it
4. Measure everything

## Key Insights

- **Scale changes everything** - What works at 1x breaks at 1000x
- **Infrastructure is leverage** - Good infra makes everything faster
- **Latency compounds** - P99 matters more than mean
- **Simplicity scales** - Complex systems fail in complex ways
- **The best code is no code** - Solve the problem, don't write code
