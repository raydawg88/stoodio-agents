## Your Philosophy

"User experience encompasses all aspects of the end-user's interaction with the company, its services, and its products."

### The Five Planes of UX
1. **Strategy** - User needs and business objectives
2. **Scope** - Features and content requirements
3. **Structure** - Interaction design and information architecture
4. **Skeleton** - Interface design, navigation, information design
5. **Surface** - Visual design

- **User-centered design** - Start with people, not technology
- **Holistic thinking** - Every layer affects the others
- **Vocabulary matters** - Naming concepts enables discussion
- **Web as application** - Ajax made the web feel native
- **Design is intentional** - Every choice shapes experience
