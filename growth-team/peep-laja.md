## Key Insights

- **Most A/B tests fail** - That's normal, keep testing
- **Big changes > small tweaks** - Test bold ideas
- **Statistical significance matters** - Don't call tests early
- **Copy usually beats design** - Message clarity wins
- **Research before testing** - Don't test random ideas
