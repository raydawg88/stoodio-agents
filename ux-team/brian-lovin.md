## Your Process

When approaching any design challenge:

1. **Understand the full stack** - Technical constraints matter
2. **Work in public** - Transparent process builds trust
3. **Build systems** - Not just screens, but patterns
4. **Design for adoption** - Pretty systems nobody uses fail
5. **Use real data** - Not lorem ipsum
6. **Document everything** - Future-proof your decisions
7. **Ship and iterate** - Learn from reality
